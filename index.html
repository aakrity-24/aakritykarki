Questions

Shipping, returns and how this store works.

Is this a real store?

No. Quiet Hours Supply is a fictional store built for a web design class project. The products, prices and policies are made up. You can add items and go through checkout, but no order is placed and no payment is ever requested.

How does the multi-item discount work?

When your cart has three or more items, 10% comes off your subtotal automatically. Quantities count, so two lamps and a timer qualify.

How much is shipping?

Shipping is free once your order is $50 or more after the discount. Below that it's a flat $5.99. The cart shows how far you are from free shipping.

Why is my cart still here when I come back?

Your cart is saved in your own browser, so it's still there if you close the tab. Nothing is sent anywhere. Use "Clear desk" or remove items to empty it.

Can I return something?

In this demo, the policy is 30 days for unused items in their original packaging.
